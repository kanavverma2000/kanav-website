Background

What I actually do.

Master of Engineering Science in Renewable Energy from UNSW Sydney, with a Google Advanced Data Analytics Professional Certificate. Engineering fundamentals plus data-driven decision-making, aimed at turning complex energy problems into recommendations people can act on.

I build tools that make Australia's electricity market legible. ARIA is the culmination — a platform over 24.5 million hourly generation records and seventeen years of prices, which reproduces AEMO's published quarterly numbers before it makes a claim of its own, and publishes its error bands and its failed validations alongside its results.

The discipline matters more than the stack. A figure read off a rendered chart is not a figure from the model; a documented fix is a hypothesis until it is measured. Most of what is on the research page exists because something I believed turned out to be wrong and I wrote down why.
