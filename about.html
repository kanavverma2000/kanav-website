Background

What I actually do.

I work two sides of the same problem: delivering renewable consulting engagements, and building the tooling that makes them faster and better evidenced.

Right now that's Renewable Project Engineer at GSES in Waterloo — Solar PV, BESS and EV charging infrastructure for federal, state and corporate clients, plus the data-science and automation workstream that modernises how the team delivers.

ARIA is the other side. Built from a blank page: live ETL off AEMO NEMWeb, a Streamlit v1, then a Next.js/FastAPI backend over a precomputed DuckDB layer. 18+ modules across capture pricing, curtailment decomposition, battery revenue stacking and coal closure scenarios, over 500+ assets.

Before that: market intelligence for developers and asset managers at Energy Synapse, fleet and EV-transition analytics across a 3,000-asset fleet at FedEx, and grid-stability work on TransGrid's NSW network through Aurecon.

A figure read off a rendered chart is not a figure from the model. A documented fix is a hypothesis until it's measured. Most of what's on the research page exists because something I believed turned out to be wrong and I wrote down why.

Forward capture scenarios to 2031
