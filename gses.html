01 — Ops platform Live

Inspection Pipeline Tool — the flagship.

End-to-end management for national solar and battery inspections. Homeowner consent, dispatch, inspector scheduling and matching, a live status board, SafetyCulture sync, ops dashboards, reconciliation against the Clean Energy Regulator's records, the inspector registry, the report pipeline, and CER portal form-fill.

Web, mobile and API. It powers national CER residential battery and PV inspections plus the QLD Battery Booster program.

How it grew. v0.3 CER/SRES compliance → v0.4 GPS and geocoding → v0.5 CEC product validation and PDF reports → v0.8 SafetyCulture ingest, reconciliation and the CER filler. Then a template registry, unified inspector profiles, a dispatch matching engine, a mobile "My Jobs" view with a dynamic form renderer, bulk email campaigns, three-way login with inspector self-application, the QLD SSFR portal, and go-live on Railway.

02 — Automation / RPA Pre-production

CER Form Filler — 150 fields, never submitted.

Browser automation that opens a Clean Energy Regulator draft submission and fills every field from a single canonical inspection record, PV and battery. Around 150 fields that were being keyed by hand, per site.

It never clicks Submit. That is a design constraint, not an oversight — a human reviews the filled form and submits it. Regulatory submissions are not somewhere to put a fully autonomous agent.

How it grew. Born inside the pipeline tool, then split out. Field maps grounded in three real SafetyCulture templates, attach-to-existing-browser so two-factor auth is never replayed, self-healing locators, screenshots at each step. Validated read-only against 50 real draft submissions before anything was written.

03 — Reporting / BI Built

QLD Government SSFR reporting — written by code, not by hand.

Reporting for Supercharged Solar for Renters, run under the Queensland Government Energy Programs Inspectorate. Three pages: programme overview with the scheduling pipeline and LGA spread, contractor and equipment allocation, and inspection outcomes with pre- and post-rectification tranche matrices.

The unusual part is that the report isn't clicked together. Two Python scripts author the data model, the report JSON, the theme and the logo programmatically, so the whole thing is reproducible and diffable rather than a binary somebody has to remember how to rebuild.

Why that matters. Government reporting recurs on a schedule. A hand-built report decays the moment the person who made it is unavailable; a generated one regenerates.

04 — Sales / CRM Delivered

Sales automation — with the guardrails in the code.

An account-based workflow: seed the CRM with target companies, confirm decision-makers manually, enrich business emails, and draft personalised intro emails into Outlook. The CRM stays the system of record and everything is logged against the organisation and contact.

Three guarantees are enforced in code rather than in a policy document: no automated LinkedIn actions, no auto-send, nothing leaves as a draft you haven't read. Outreach tooling that can send on its own is a reputational liability, and the constraint belongs where it can't be forgotten.

Delivered as a self-contained web app with idempotent CRM setup and credit-guarded enrichment, packaged and handed to the Managing Director.

05 — Desktop utility Packaged

Photo evidence tool — defensible metadata.

A desktop app that corrects and stamps capture timestamps and applies watermarks to battery inspection photos, so field evidence carries consistent, defensible metadata when it goes to the regulator.

Small problem, real consequence: an inspection photo whose timestamp can't be trusted is an inspection photo that can be challenged. Handles HEIC and HEIF alongside JPG and PNG, because that's what phones actually produce.

How it grew. A metadata-amending script that turned into a full GUI with an auto-installer, then bundled into a one-click Windows executable with desktop shortcuts so inspectors never see a terminal.
