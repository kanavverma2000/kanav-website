What We Found

We Made Them Talk.

CURTAILMENT — VERIFIED ASSET DATA

Manildra Solar: 82.3% curtailed. $884K gone.

ARIA tracks curtailment for every asset. Manildra runs at just 5.3% capacity factor despite being grid-connected. Culcairn Solar (466 MW) lost 127,266 MWh and $6.85M in revenue. SA assets face a double hit: 36% negative pricing and 50%+ curtailment.

CAPTURE RATES — 144 ASSETS BENCHMARKED

Darling Downs Solar captures 13.8¢ per dollar. Capital Wind gets $1.06.

Across 94 solar assets, median capture ratio is 0.42x — meaning solar earns 42 cents for every dollar of average price. Wind median is 0.71x. Capital Wind Farm hit 105.6% capture in 2024 — beating the market. Darling Downs Solar captures just 13.8%, with 56% of its MWh during negative prices.

BATTERY ECONOMICS — 44 BESS TRACKED

Templers BESS: $287/MWh spread. Bungama: negative $137.

Median battery arbitrage spread is $173/MWh. Templers BESS leads at $287/MWh — charging at negative prices, discharging at peaks. But Bungama BESS runs backwards: buying at $137/MWh, selling at $0.42. Eraring BESS (700 MW) is now Australia's largest. ARIA tracks every cycle.

NEGATIVE PRICING — REGIONAL BREAKDOWN

Victoria: 43.1% of dispatch intervals are negative-priced.

In Q4 2025, VIC saw negative prices in nearly half of all dispatch intervals. SA isn't far behind. Wholesale prices crashed 44% year-on-year to $50/MWh nationally. Wollar Solar Farm posted a negative revenue month in September 2025 — generating 48,214 MWh and losing $56K.

THE PIPELINE BOTTLENECK

64 GW queued. Only 4.4 GW delivered per year.

The NEM connection queue has 64 GW of projects waiting vs a throughput of 4.4 GW/year. That's a 14-year backlog. Golden Plains (730 MW), Wandoona Solar (800 MW), Oven Mountain Pumped Hydro (600 MW) — all waiting. ARIA tracks every project from DA to COD.

REGIONAL INVESTMENT REALITY

NSW scores 10/10 on depth. SA is storage-only. TAS is a niche.

ARIA's market reality model scores every NEM region on market depth, policy certainty, contracting support, and scale viability. NSW leads overall. VIC is strategic long-term but faces severe near-term pain. SA's solar capture-rate collapse means only hybrid/storage projects pencil. The NEM now punishes simplistic merchant exposure.

Every number above comes from ARIA's own analysis of AEMO dispatch data, NEMWeb feeds, and CSIRO benchmarks.
