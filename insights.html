How I write them

Rules I set after
reading my own data.

I pulled the engagement on all 59 posts and let it decide the format rather than guessing. Some of what it said was unflattering.

What the data said to do
01
Open with a bare Australian figure. Number alone on line one, named on line two. Australian-number opens averaged 2,617 impressions against 577 for foreign ones — about 4.5×.
02
Provenance first, number second, mechanism third. "I processed 5.1 million dispatch intervals to answer one question." That line is the whole difference between analysis and commentary.
03
Name real assets, DUIDs and constraint IDs. Manildra, Coopers Gap, LD01–04, Q_NIL_STRGTH_CKWF. Specificity is the credibility.
04
Close with one question I genuinely can't answer from my own data, addressed to a specific role. The post that did that and credited a named person got 25 comments on 1,383 impressions — my highest engagement rate.
05
Publish the negative result. Including my own. Almost nobody in this market does, and it consistently reads above median.
What the data said to stop
  • Foreign-number cold opens. My four worst posts all opened with a Germany, UK, Texas or California figure. Average 6 reactions. The material is fine — as a second beat, never the first line.
  • Hashtag stuffing. 20–30 per post, some mid-sentence, which breaks the prose. My highest-reach post used 8. Now 3–5, at the end.
  • Generic closers. "Any thoughts?" does nothing when no real question is being asked.
  • Curiosity-gap hooks. "Here's what the ISP isn't telling you." My own data doesn't reward them either.
  • Mid-sentence caps and multiple exclamation marks. Sharp is on-voice. Melodramatic isn't.
  • Publishing the same post twice. I did that once. Process failure, not style.
The standing rule Re-derive every figure the morning it goes out. No placeholder numbers, ever. A figure read off a rendered chart is not a figure from the model — I learned that when a curtailment projection I published at 84% turned out to come from a hard-coded array while the model said 78.4%.
